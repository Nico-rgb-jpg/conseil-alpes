no ip domain-lookup

! --- VLAN (crees a la main sur chaque switch, VTP en mode transparent) ---

vlan 10

vlan 20

vlan 30

vlan 40
 name INVITES
vlan 50

vlan 99

 description Trunk vers R1 G0/0

 switchport nonegotiate

 exit

 description Trunk vers SW-ETG1
